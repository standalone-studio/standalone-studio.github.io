About Paper

Building tools that empower creativity, one app at a time.

Our Story

What is Paper?

Paper is a growing ecosystem of creative applications built with a single philosophy: technology should feel natural, beautiful, and empowering. Each app in the Paper family is designed to strip away complexity and let you focus on what matters most: your ideas.

From note-taking to painting, from reading to music creation, Paper brings a consistent, premium experience across every touchpoint.

The Team

Standalone Studio

Paper is crafted by Standalone Studio, an independent development studio passionate about building high-quality digital products. We believe in clean design, thoughtful engineering, and respect for our users.

Every pixel, every interaction, every line of code is a reflection of our commitment to excellence.
